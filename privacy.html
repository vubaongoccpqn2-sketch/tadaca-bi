Privacy Policy – TADACA BI

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Who we are

TADACA BI is an internal reporting tool operated by TADACA (tadaca.vn), Vietnam.

2. Data we access

Only after the TikTok account owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, we access: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), account statistics (follower, following, likes and video counts) and the list of public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

3. How we use the data

The data is used solely to build internal marketing reports for TADACA. We do not sell, rent or share it with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising targeting.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in TADACA's private Google Workspace (Google Sheets / Google Apps Script). Access tokens are kept in private script properties and are never published.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for reporting. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions); after revocation we stop collecting data and delete stored tokens.

6. Contact

For privacy questions please contact TADACA via tadaca.vn.
