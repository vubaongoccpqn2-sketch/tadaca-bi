Terms of Service – TADACA BI

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Service

TADACA BI is an internal tool of TADACA (tadaca.vn) that reads statistics of TikTok accounts owned by TADACA and writes them to private reports.

2. Eligible users

Only TADACA staff authorized by the company may use the tool, and only with TikTok accounts that TADACA owns or manages.

3. Authorization

By connecting a TikTok account, the user confirms they have the right to grant access to that account. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings.

4. Acceptable use

The tool must be used in compliance with the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Developer Terms. Data obtained must not be sold or shared outside TADACA.

5. Disclaimer

The tool is provided "as is" for internal reporting. TADACA may change or discontinue it at any time.
