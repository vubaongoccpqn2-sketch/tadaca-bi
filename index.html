TADACA BI

TADACA BI is an internal reporting tool of TADACA (Vietnam). It connects to TADACA's own TikTok account with the owner's permission and copies channel statistics (follower count, likes, video list and video view/like/comment/share counts) into a private Google Sheet for internal marketing reports.

The tool is not offered to the public and is used only by TADACA staff.
